Bhagat Singh

1907 - 1931

The Great Martyr of India

Bhagat Singh Bhagat Singh was born on 28 September 1907 in Banga village, Lyallpur district (now in Pakistan). He was born into a patriotic Sikh family—freedom, was in his blood.His father Kishan Singh and uncles Ajit Singh and Swaran Singh were actively involved in the freedom struggle.Growing up, Bhagat Singh didn’t dream of a quiet life. From childhood, he spoke openly about driving the British out of India. That kind of clarity at such a young age is rare.

Biographies

  • Inspired by the Jallianwala Bagh massacre (1919), which strengthened his revolutionary spirit.
  • Studied at National College, Lahore, where he developed nationalist and socialist ideas.
  • Joined the Hindustan Republican Association (HRA), later renamed HSRA.
  • Participated in the Saunders assassination (1928) to avenge the death of Lala Lajpat Rai.
  • Led a 116-day hunger strike in jail demanding equal rights for Indian political prisoners.
  • Executed by the British on 23 March 1931 along with Rajguru and Sukhdev.
  • Attained martyrdom at the age of 23 and earned the title Shaheed-e-Azam.
  • Remembered as a symbol of bravery, patriotism, and youth inspiration.
